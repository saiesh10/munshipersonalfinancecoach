## Brand & Style

This design system draws direct inspiration from the timeless discipline of traditional Indian mercantile bookkeeping (the *bahi-khata*). It treats personal financial stewardship not as an abstract dashboard of floating cards, but as an enduring, deliberate craft recorded with ink on heavy textured paper.

The aesthetic fuses disciplined editorial rigor with the tactile dignity of archival parchment. The tone is measured, respectful, authoritative, and deeply human. Interacting with the system should evoke the sensation of sitting down with an ancestral family munshi: clear-eyed accountability, quiet reassurance, and zero room for decorative vanity.

The UI rejects digital gloss, floating drop-shadows, synthetic gradients, and playful curved geometry. Structure is built purely through crisp 1px hairline rules, precise typographic scales, tabular column alignment, and the deliberate contrast of hand-ruled margins against parchment grounds.

## Colors

The color palette is modeled strictly on the physical artifacts of an accountant’s desk: cotton-pressed paper, aged wood-pulp slips, carbon-black gall ink, herbal moss stamping ink, and crushed marigold ceremonial pigment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Paper Canvas (`#F3EEE3`)**: The unbleached ground representing primary background canvas. Light, matte, and comfortable for prolonged contemplation.
- **Deep Paper / Ledger Surface (`#EAE3D3`)**: A deeper, tinted surface tone used to distinguish secondary sections, tabular rows, inset panels, and summary ledgers.
- **Ink Primary (`#1C1B18`)**: Deep iron-carbon black. Used for high-priority numbers, body text, headlines, and structural rules.
- **Ink Soft (`#5C574D`)**: Diluted sepia-charcoal ink. Reserved for secondary annotations, contextual metadata, timestamps, and column titles.
- **Rule / Hairline Border (`#CFC7B4`)**: The color of hand-scribed ruled ledger lines. Defines all structural frames, separators, and grid boundaries.
- **Marigold (`#C8651B`)**: Saffron-tinted earth accent. Designates callouts, fiscal warnings, targets, projections, and motivational "Future You" interventions.
- **Moss (`#3F6B4A`)**: Pressed botanical green. Encodes ledger credits, surplus balances, confirmed settlements, and disciplined fiscal status.

### Usage Directives
- **Zero Gradients**: Never use chromatic or luminance gradients. All fills must be opaque, solid, and flat.
- **Contrast**: Pure `#1C1B18` ink sits directly on `#F3EEE3` or `#EAE3D3`. No semi-transparent opacities for text; use `#5C574D` for muted contrast.

## Typography

Typography embodies the balance between narrative counsel and mathematical certainty.

- **EB Garamond** acts as the voice of the Munshi: warm, literary, humanistic, and dignified. It is utilized across editorial commentary, advisory narratives, narrative headers, and philosophical reflections on saving and spending.
- **JetBrains Mono** represents the immutable discipline of the ledger: strictly monospaced, crisp, and mechanical. It commands all financial sums (Rupees `₹`), ledger account codes, tabular ledgers, metadata tags, dates, and category kickers.

### Rules of Typesetting
- All fiscal balances, numeric percentages, and transaction entries **must** be rendered in `JetBrains Mono` with tabular lining figures.
- Use the uppercase `kicker` role (letter-spacing `0.12em`) above headers to categorize ledger entries, sections, and dates (e.g., `// KHATA NO. 402 — FISCAL SUMMARY`).
- Avoid mixing serif numbers inside running financial tables; numerals in the ledger are always monospace.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column editorial grid on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 2 columns on mobile. Structural spacing mimics traditional printed bookkeeping sheets, where data is bounded by vertical folio lines and horizontal accounting margins.

### Grid & Margins
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column grid with a fixed maximum container of `1200px`. Outer margin is `2.5rem` (`margin-desktop`) and column gutter is `1.5rem` (`gutter-desktop`).
- **Tablet (640px – 1023px)**: 6-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 2-column or single-stream layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` column gutters.

### Ledger Padding System
Elements do not float freely. Every container, cell, and list item is locked to an exact grid rhythm:
- Inset padding for cells: `0.75rem` vertically, `1rem` horizontally.
- Dividers between ledger rows are strictly 1px borders colored `#CFC7B4`.
- Column alignments must be uncompromising: Text descriptions align left; numeric financial figures align strictly to the right.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system contains **zero blur-based drop shadows** and **zero z-index hovering layers**. Digital elevation is considered anachronistic to the ledger motif. Depth is achieved exclusively through tonal stacking, border framing, and hairline ruled layers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground (Canvas)**: `#F3EEE3` (Paper). The physical desk and primary open page.
- **Layer 1 (Folio / Ledger Surface)**: `#EAE3D3` (Deep Paper). Used for inset statement blocks, total balance summaries, and active data tables. Visually separated by a `1px solid #CFC7B4` border.
- **Layer 2 (Accented Focus / Active Note)**: `#F3EEE3` nested inside `#EAE3D3` framed with a double-rule (`border: 3px double #1C1B18`) or offset hairline rule (`1px solid #1C1B18`).
- **Modal / Dialog Sheets**: Never use blurred backdrops (`backdrop-filter`). Modals are opaque full-contrast paper panels bordered by a 2px solid `#1C1B18` stroke, resting atop an opaque `#1C1B18` scrim at 30% density.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Elements have sharp, right-angled 90-degree corners (`0px` border-radius), reflecting the precision of hand-trimmed parchment, physical index cards, and printed stationery. 

Under no circumstances should rounded capsules, circular icon holders, or pill-shaped tags appear. In contexts where software requires visual softening (such as interactive states), an absolute maximum radius of `1px` may be tolerated, but default production remains strictly unrounded (`0px`).

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1C1B18` fill, `#F3EEE3` text, `0px` radius. Monospace kicker or label typography (`label-md`). Hover state shifts to `#5C574D` with no transition lag.
- **Secondary / Ledger Action**: Outlined button with `1px solid #1C1B18`, transparent fill, `#1C1B18` text. Hover reverses to `#EAE3D3` fill.
- **Editorial Action (Text link)**: Ink text underlined with a `1px solid #1C1B18` rule offset by `3px`.

### Ledger Cards & Data Panes
- Rectangular blocks with `#EAE3D3` or `#F3EEE3` background.
- Bordered on all four sides by `1px solid #CFC7B4`.
- Header bars within cards are separated by a continuous horizontal rule (`1px solid #CFC7B4`) with a label kicker in `JetBrains Mono` (`#5C574D`).
- For primary advisory summaries, apply a left accent border: `3px solid #C8651B` (Marigold) for guidance or `3px solid #3F6B4A` (Moss) for cleared ledger summaries.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Flat `#F3EEE3` surface with a bottom-only rule (`1px solid #5C574D`) or complete `1px solid #CFC7B4` perimeter. No rounded corners. Focus state thickens the bottom rule to `2px solid #1C1B18`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square boxes (`0px` radius), `14px` by `14px` with a `1px solid #1C1B18` border. When checked, the box contains a solid square fill or crisp monospace `[X]` / `✓` mark in `#1C1B18`.

### Ledger Table (The Core Bahi-Khata)
- Table headers are uppercase `kicker` font with a bottom double hairline (`3px double #CFC7B4`).
- Rows alternate between `#F3EEE3` and `#EAE3D3` or remain unified with `1px solid #CFC7B4` row borders.
- Debit / Expense amounts: `#1C1B18` with an explicit minus prefix (`- ₹1,450.00`).
- Credit / Income amounts: `#3F6B4A` with an explicit plus prefix (`+ ₹24,000.00`).
- Ledger Notes / Category: `EB Garamond` italic body.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- Strict rectangular tags (`0px` radius).
- Background `#EAE3D3`, border `1px solid #CFC7B4`, text `label-md` in `#5C574D`.
- Status chips use subtle ink color coding: Moss border and text for settled entries; Marigold border and text for pending audits or warnings.

### Munshi Advisory Callout ("Future You")
- Dedicated editorial callout container.
- Background `#EAE3D3` with a top and bottom hairline rule (`1px solid #C8651B`).
- Accompanied by a monospaced label `// MUNSHI ADVISORY` in `#C8651B`, followed by guidance set in `EB Garamond` italic.
